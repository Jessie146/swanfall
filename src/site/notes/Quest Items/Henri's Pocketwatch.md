**Henri's Pocket Watch** is a silver pocket watch belonging to [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Henri Beaumont\|Henri Beaumont]], a gentleman of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. The watch was passed down to Henri by his father and holds considerable sentimental value despite having little monetary worth.

>[!Quote |mark] [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Henri Beaumont\|Henri Beaumont]]
>It belonged to my father. I don't care what it's worth. I simply want it back.

#### **Overview**

**Henri's Pocket Watch** is a small silver watch with a simple, understated design. Although it is valuable enough to attract the attention of a street thief, it is not particularly remarkable as an object. Its importance comes from its history rather than its craftsmanship.

#### **Effects** 

The pocket watch has no practical use to the party.

It can be returned to [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Henri Beaumont\|Henri Beaumont]] during [[Quests/Suereé Quests/The Missing Pocket Watch\|The Missing Pocket Watch]]

#### **Current Location** 

Possessed by [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Henri Beaumont\|Henri Beaumont]].
