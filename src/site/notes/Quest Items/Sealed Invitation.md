#### **Overview**

The invitation is crafted from unusually thick black parchment trimmed with faded gold leaf. The edges are slightly warped, as though exposed to moisture during transport, and the paper carries a faint scent of expensive perfume mixed with damp canal water. Sealing the fold is a circle of dark wax embossed with the image of a cathedral bell wrapped in thorned vines, a symbol unfamiliar to most outside [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]].

#### **Trivia**
- The invitation bears no date.
- No noble crest or family signature appears anywhere on the parchment.
- Those familiar with **Suerré’s** elite circles immediately recognize the invitation as highly irregular, as legitimate noble houses always display their crest openly.
