[[Reputation/Suerré Reputation Token\|Suerré Reputation Token]] are a form of [[Reputation\|Reputation]] awarded to the player for completing quests and performing actions that influence the city of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. They represent the party's growing recognition among the city's inhabitants, institutions, and factions.

Reputation Tokens contribute to the party's overall standing in Suerré. As this standing increases, the party may gain access to new dialogue options, local contacts, discounts, and quests. Reputation may also be affected by choices that benefit one faction while damaging the interests of another.

#### Earning Reputation

Reputation Tokens are primarily awarded for completing quests and making decisions that benefit Suerré or its inhabitants.

|Activity|Reputation|
|---|---|
|Completing a minor local quest|+1–3|
|Completing a major quest|+5–10|
|Helping a local merchant|+2|
|Assisting a faction|+5|
|Resolving a conflict peacefully|+2–5|
|Making a significant contribution to the city|+10|
|Damaging the interests of Suerre|−1–5|

#### Reputation Ranks

[[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]] Reputation is measured on a scale of 0 to 100. As the party accumulates [[Reputation/Suerré Reputation\|Suerré Reputation]], they progress through a series of Reputation Ranks.

| Rank        | Reputation | Description                                                                   |
| ----------- | ---------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inconnu     |        0–9 | The party is largely unknown in Suerré                                        |
| Connu       |      10–24 | The party has begun to make a name for itself.                                |
| Recommandé  |      25–49 | The party is vouched for by others and has established a positive reputation. |
| Respecté    |      50–74 | The party is regarded as an important and respected presence in the city.     |
| Bienfaiteur |     75–100 | The party is recognised for its significant contributions to Suerré.          |
#### Reputation Benefits

As the party's reputation increases, they may gain access to new opportunities within Suerré.

| Rank        | Reputation | Description                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------- | ---------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Inconnu     |        0–9 | The party has little influence in the city. Most NPCs treat them as strangers, and few special opportunities are available.                                                                            |
| Connu       |      10–24 | The party begins to establish a name for itself. Certain locals may acknowledge previous deeds, and minor opportunities may become available.                                                          |
| Recommandé  |      25–49 | The party has earned the confidence of some communities. NPCs may be more willing to provide information, offer assistance, or introduce the party to others.                                          |
| Respecté    |      50–74 | The party is regarded as an important ally within Suerré. New quests, faction opportunities, and special interactions may become available.                                                            |
| Bienfaiteur |     75–100 | The party has achieved a significant reputation within the city. Their actions carry considerable weight, and they may gain access to unique opportunities unavailable to less recognised adventurers. |
