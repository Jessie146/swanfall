Suerre Reputation Tokens are small brass chits awarded to individuals who have rendered notable service to the city of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. Each token bears the seal of the city and serves as a recognised mark of civic merit.

Tokens are awarded for completing quests and performing actions that benefit the city's inhabitants. They contribute to the holder's [[Reputation/Suerré Reputation\|Suerré Reputation]] and may be exchanged for certain local favours or privileges.

#### Appearance

A Suerre Reputation Token is a small, circular brass disc, stamped with the city's seal. The reverse bears a simple inscription: 

> Pour le bien de Suerré 
> »  For the good of Suerré.

#### Acquisition

Reputation Tokens are primarily obtained through quests and notable acts of service within Suerre.
