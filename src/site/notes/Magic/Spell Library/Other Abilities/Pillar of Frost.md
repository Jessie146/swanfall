#### Overview

**Pillar of Frost** is a defensive ice-based spell that conjures a solid column of magically reinforced ice on a targeted tile. It is commonly used for battlefield control, obstruction, and temporary fortification.

When cast, the spell summons a towering pillar of frost that occupies a single tile. The structure is opaque, durable, and immobile, preventing all movement and blocking both physical and magical attacks through its space. The pillar persists for a limited duration or until destroyed.

When [[System/Overcast\|Overcast]], **Pillar of Frost** expands its effect to form a short wall of ice spanning two adjacent tiles. This enhanced version, **Wall of Frost**, provides greater area denial and can be strategically placed to block corridors, protect allies, or divide enemy forces. [[System/Overcast\|Overcast]] failure freezes the user.

#### Effects

**Primary Effect**

- Forms a column of ice over one tile blocking [[System/Movement\|Movement]] and [[System/Line of Sight\|Line of Sight]].

** Overcast Effect**
- 50% chance - Transforms to **Wall of Ice** covering two adjacent tiles blocking [[System/Movement\|Movement]] and [[System/Line of Sight\|Line of Sight]].
- 30% chance - Transforms to **Wall of Ice** covering two adjacent tiles blocking [[System/Movement\|Movement]] and [[System/Line of Sight\|Line of Sight]], and causes the user to become [[Status Effects/Frozen\|Frozen]] for one turn.
- 20% chance - Causes the user to become [[Status Effects/Frozen\|Frozen]] for one turn.

#### Limitations

- Cannot be cast on occupied tiles.
- Vulnerable to fire-based or shattering effects.
- Limited duration prevents permanent terrain alteration.

#### Trivia

- In colder regions, the pillar’s duration is rumoured to last slightly longer, as ambient temperatures reinforce the magic.
- Some veteran spellweavers intentionally shatter their own pillar to create a burst of ice shards, using it offensively.
- The pillar emits a faint cracking sound, even when undamaged, caused by magical tension within the ice.
