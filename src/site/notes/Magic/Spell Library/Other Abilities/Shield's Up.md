#### Overview

**Shield's Up** is a defensive spell commonly practiced across multiple magical disciplines within the world. Designed as a rapid-response incantation, it manifests a protective barrier around the caster, offering both physical and arcane resistance. The spell is favoured by battlemages, traveling sorcerers, and defensive specialists due to its versatility and relatively quick casting time.

#### Effects

**Primary Effect**

- Restore 3 [[System/Armour\|Magic Armour]] 
- Restore 3 [[System/Armour\|Physical Armour]] 

**Overcast Effect**
- 50% chance - Transforms to **Wall's Up** restoring 6 [[System/Armour\|Magic Armour]] and [[System/Armour\|Physical Armour]].
- 30% chance - Transforms to **Wall's Up** restoring 6 [[System/Armour\|Magic Armour]] and [[System/Armour\|Physical Armour]] and causes the user to become [[Status Effects/Stunned\|Stunned]] for one turn.
- 20% chance - Causes the user to become [[Status Effects/Stunned\|Stunned]] for one turn.

#### Limitations

- Can only be cast on self
- Forgoes an offensive action to use

#### Trivia

- **Shield's Up** is often one of the first defensive spells taught to novice mages.
- Some elite warriors train to cast **Shield's Up** reflexively, activating it in moments of sudden danger without conscious thought.
