Street Tricks is a collection of deceptive techniques mastered by Sueree thieves, confidence artists, and pickpockets. Though not true magic, these tricks are so practiced they often appear supernatural. 

#### Effects 

Choose one of the following each time Street Tricks is cast: 
**Shadowstep** Move up to 3 metres to an unoccupied space adjacent to cover without provoking opportunity attacks. 
**Pocket Sand** A creature within 3 metres must make an Agility save or become [[Status Effects/Blinded\|Blinded]] until the end of its next turn. 
**False Tell** Select one creature within 6 metres. It gains [[Distracted\|Distracted]], suffering **-1** to its next attack or perception check. 
**Quick Lift** Attempt to steal a Small carried item, key, coin pouch, or document from a creature within melee range. If successful, the target does not notice until the end of its next turn. 
**Blend In** If adjacent to a crowd, alley, market stall, or similar cover, gain [[Status Effects/Hidden\|Hidden]] until you attack or leave cover.
