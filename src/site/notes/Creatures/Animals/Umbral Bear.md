The Umbral Bear is a large shadow-infused predator native to the [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]]. Though rarely seen, it is feared for its ability to blend into darkness and is central to the events of the quest [[Quests/Silverleaf Woods Quests/The Thorne In My Side\|The Thorne In My Side]].
