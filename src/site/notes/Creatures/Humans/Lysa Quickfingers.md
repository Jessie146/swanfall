Lysa "Quickfingers" is a Suereé pickpocket operating throughout the back alleys and market districts of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]], the City of Endless Night. Though regarded by the city watch as a common thief, Lysa possesses an extensive network of contacts among beggars, smugglers, and street performers. During the events of [[Quests/Suereé Quests/Where the Nightingale Sings\|Where the Nightingale Sings]], the player must decide whether to pursue, bargain with, or fight the elusive thief.

Lysa is known for stealing only what can be carried unnoticed and for never drawing a blade unless cornered. Among the poorer districts she has gained a quiet reputation for redistributing portions of her earnings to orphaned Suereé children, making her difficult for the authorities to apprehend.
