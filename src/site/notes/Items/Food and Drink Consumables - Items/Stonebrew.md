#### **Overview**

**Stonebrew** is a heavy, dark beer brewed deep within mountain forges using subterranean spring water and iron-rich grains. Unlike ordinary ales, it carries a faint grit and metallic aftertaste, a hallmark of its unique brewing process.

It is sold by vendors and taverns across the realm as the drink of choice.

#### **Effects**

**Primary Effect**

- Restores **2 Vitality Points**
    

**Secondary Effect**

- Applies [[Status Effects/Stone Haze\|Stone Haze]]:
    
    - -10% Accuracy
        
    - Duration: 2 turns
        

Upon consumption, **Stonebrew** restores **2 Vitality Points**, making it a common choice for quick recovery in the field. However, its thickness and intoxicating qualities reduce the drinker’s precision, causing a **10% decrease in Accuracy for 2 turns**. The loss of accuracy is often described as a slight heaviness in the limbs and a dulling of focus, as if the drinker’s senses are “weighed down by stone.”

#### **Variants**

- [[Items/Food and Drink Consumables - Items/Refined Stonebrew\|Refined Stonebrew]] – Filtered for smoother taste; reduced accuracy penalty
    
- [[Items/Food and Drink Consumables - Items/Iron Reserve\|Iron Reserve]] – Stronger variant with increased vitality restoration but harsher penalties
    
- [[Items/Food and Drink Consumables - Items/Traveler’s Blend\|Traveler’s Blend]] – Diluted version favoured by non-dwarves

#### **Trivia**

- Non-dwarves often describe their first experience with **Stonebrew** as “drinking liquid gravel.”
    
- Some warriors deliberately use **Stonebrew** before defensive engagements, valuing resilience over precision.
    
- It is nearly impossible to spill when poured properly due to its viscosity.
