The **Phase Duellist** has abandoned the Riftblade's constant movement in favour of mastering the space between themselves and a single opponent. By manipulating the dimensions surrounding their target, a Phase Duelist can make an enemy appear impossibly close, impossibly far away, or unable to escape their reach.

They are particularly dangerous against **elite enemies, champions, and other powerful single targets**.

#### Phase Duellist Mechanics

##### Phase Mark

The Phase Duelist can place a **Phase Mark** on an enemy. Many abilities become stronger against the marked target. The Phase Duellist can also use the mark to manipulate their position relative to the target.

##### Duel Space

Certain abilities create a temporary **Duel Space** around the Phase Duellist and their marked enemy.

While inside Duel Space:
- The Phase Duellist gains increased damage against the marked target.
- Dimensional attacks ignore additional armour.
- The marked enemy has reduced ability to escape.
- Other enemies provide fewer opportunities for the Duellist to change targets.

#### Signature Abilities

**[[Phase Mark\|Phase Mark]]**

Mark an enemy for several turns. The Phase Duellist deals increased damage against the marked target.

**[[Through the Guard\|Through the Guard]]**

Strike directly through the target's dimensional space. The attack ignores a large portion of Physical Armour.

**[[Mirror Parry\|Mirror Parry]]**

When the marked enemy attacks the Phase Duellist, briefly shift into another dimension, avoiding the attack and immediately counterattacking.

**[[Close the Distance\|Close the Distance]]**

Teleport directly beside the marked target regardless of intervening creatures or terrain.

**[[Finality\|Finality]]**

The Phase Duellist collapses the space surrounding their opponent and performs a devastating dimensional strike. The ability deals additional damage based on how long the target has remained Phase Marked.
