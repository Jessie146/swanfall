**Stagehand Émile** is a veteran stage technician employed by the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]] in [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. Responsible for operating the theatre's elaborate scenery, lighting systems, and hidden mechanisms, Émile inadvertently becomes a key witness to the murder that occurs during [[Quests/Suereé Quests/Where the Nightingale Sings\|Where the Nightingale Sings]]. His disappearance sparks the investigation central to [[Quests/Suereé Quests/A Song for the Guilty\|A Song for the Guilty]], where the party must locate him before those responsible for the killing can ensure his silence.

> [!Quote |mark] [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Stagehand Émile\|Stagehand Émile]]  
> "People come to the theatre to watch the stage. They never think to look above it."

#### Biography

Émile has worked behind the curtains of the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]] for over twenty years, becoming intimately familiar with every rope, pulley, hidden corridor, and forgotten maintenance tunnel concealed within the historic opera house. Though little known beyond the theatre's staff, he is widely respected among performers for his meticulous attention to detail and uncanny ability to solve mechanical failures moments before a performance begins.

Unlike the actors and singers who bask in applause, Émile has spent his career ensuring audiences never notice his work. He oversees scene changes, maintains the theatre's ageing machinery, and coordinates crews high above the stage from the network of wooden catwalks hidden beneath the roof.

On the night of [[Quests/Suereé Quests/Where the Nightingale Sings\|Where the Nightingale Sings]], Émile witnesses a masked figure moving through the fly loft during [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]'s performance. Believing it to be an intruder, he quietly follows them across the theatre's upper walkways. By the time he reaches the noble's private balcony, the murder has already been committed.

Realising he has seen too much, Émile flees into the theatre's maintenance passages before the killer notices him. His disappearance leads the city constables to suspect he was involved in the assassination, while the true conspirators dispatch a [[Creatures/Humans/Masked Assassin\|Masked Assassin]] to eliminate him before he can testify.

The party eventually discovers Émile hiding within an abandoned scenery workshop beneath the theatre. Though terrified, his testimony provides the first reliable account of the killer's movements and reveals that the murder was carefully orchestrated long before the audience ever entered the building.

#### Relationships

> [!kith|ally] **[[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]** _Trusted Performer_

Although they rarely speak outside rehearsals, Émile has operated the stage machinery for nearly every one of the Nightingale's performances. She trusts his discretion and has quietly relied upon his knowledge of the theatre's hidden passages for years.

> [!kith|antagonist] **[[Characters/Suerre Characters/Captain Lucien Moreau\|Captain Lucien Moreau]]** _Commander of the Investigation_

Émile initially fears Captain Moreau will arrest him as an accomplice to the murder, prompting him to flee before he can give his statement.

#### Personality

Émile is cautious, humble, and deeply uncomfortable with attention. Accustomed to remaining unseen, he prefers machinery to conversation and often speaks in practical observations rather than opinions. Years spent working above crowded audiences have made him exceptionally observant, noticing details others overlook without fully appreciating their significance.

Though timid by nature, Émile possesses a quiet courage when confronted with injustice. Despite knowing powerful individuals wish him dead, he ultimately agrees to testify, believing that allowing the truth to disappear would betray everyone who has ever worked honestly within the theatre.

His intimate knowledge of the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]] makes him an invaluable guide through its concealed architecture, including forgotten rehearsal rooms, maintenance tunnels, and narrow catwalks inaccessible to most visitors.

#### Renown

Among the performers of the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]], Émile is affectionately known as **"The Ghost of the Stage,"** a nickname earned through his uncanny habit of appearing precisely where he is needed before quietly disappearing behind the scenery moments later.

Outside theatrical circles, however, few citizens of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]] know his name, despite many having unknowingly witnessed productions made possible by his work behind the curtain.

#### Trivia

- Émile claims he can identify every performer in the theatre solely by the sound of their footsteps backstage.
- He refuses to whistle while working, believing it brings bad luck to stage crews.
- He carries a brass pocket watch that has stopped at the exact minute the theatre first opened decades ago.
- He has never watched a full performance from the audience, despite spending most of his life inside the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]].
