Marcel Fournier is a baker operating the small bakery of [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/The Pâtisserie du Matin\|The Pâtisserie du Matin]] in [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. He is an ordinary and well-liked member of his neighbourhood, although his increasingly obvious frustration with [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Étienne Bellamy\|Étienne Bellamy]]'s attempts to investigate him has occasionally drawn attention.

>[!Quote |mark] [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Marcel Fournier\|Marcel Fournier]]
>"I'm a baker. I buy flour, I bake bread, and I go home. There's nothing more to it."

#### Biography

Little is known about Marcel's life beyond his work as a baker. He has operated his bakery in Suerré for several years and has become a familiar face among the surrounding residents.

#### Relationships

> [!kith|antagonist] **[[Characters/Suerre Characters/Étienne Bellamy\|Étienne Bellamy]]** _Adversary_

Étienne has spent several nights observing Marcel, convinced that his ordinary activities are evidence of a criminal conspiracy. Marcel finds the behaviour irritating and more than a little absurd.

#### Personality

Marcel is a practical, hardworking and generally good-natured man. He has little patience for nonsense and becomes particularly exasperated when people attempt to make ordinary matters more complicated than they are.

#### Renown

Marcel is known locally as a reliable baker, although he is unlikely to be recognised elsewhere in Suerré.

#### Trivia

- Marcel owns three identical aprons, much to the interest of [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Étienne Bellamy\|Étienne Bellamy]].
- His bakery closes at precisely eight o'clock each evening.
- Marcel considers being investigated by Étienne considerably more suspicious than anything he has actually done.
