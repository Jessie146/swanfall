**Rat Capone** is the undisputed leader of the [[Factions/Suereé Factions/The Ratpack\|The Ratpack]], the largest criminal syndicate operating within [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. Equal parts crime boss, merchant, and manipulator, he controls an extensive network of smugglers, fences, spies, and informants hidden beneath the city's streets and canals.

While widely known as one of the city's foremost weapons dealers, Capone's true talent lies in deciding _who_ receives those weapons. Those he wishes to prosper receive reliable steel and carefully maintained equipment. Those he wishes to see destroyed unknowingly purchase rusted blades, unstable firearms, cracked armour, or sabotaged supplies disguised as valuable merchandise.

Few realise that beneath his tailored suits and polished manners lurks a **wererat**, a secret known only to his closest lieutenants. Rumours of an enormous rat walking the tunnels beneath Suerré are dismissed by most citizens as little more than tavern folklore.

> [!Quote |mark] [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Rat Capone\|Rat Capone]]  
> "Business isn't about selling weapons. It's about deciding who lives long enough to buy another."

#### Biography

Almost nothing is known of Rat Capone before his arrival in [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. Criminal folklore claims he emerged from the city's forgotten sewer network decades ago after uniting dozens of rival wererat colonies beneath a single banner. Whether these stories contain any truth remains uncertain, though even the oldest smugglers admit the Ratpack seemed to appear almost overnight.

Unlike other crime lords who relied upon violence, Capone built his empire through commerce. He recognised that every conflict in Suerré created demand for; weapons, armour, information, forged documents, and discreet transportation. By controlling these markets simultaneously, he ensured nearly every faction eventually became indebted to him.

His most infamous practice involves the deliberate sale of defective weaponry. Capone rarely refuses a customer, regardless of allegiance, but the quality of his merchandise depends entirely upon whether he wishes them to succeed. Mercenary bands he considers unreliable may unknowingly receive swords weakened along the tang, crossbows with warped limbs, or alchemical explosives designed to fail at the worst possible moment. Should such a group perish, Capone simply purchases their remaining equipment from scavengers before selling it again.

This calculated manipulation has allowed the Ratpack to influence countless conflicts without openly participating in them. Entire gangs have vanished believing themselves betrayed by fate, never suspecting the flaw lay in the weapon they trusted.

Despite commanding one of the city's most feared criminal organisations, Capone rarely leaves the hidden chambers beneath Suerré. Meetings with outsiders are conducted in lavish underground offices where every entrance is watched by silent bodyguards and every wall conceals escape routes known only to the Ratpack.

#### Relationships

> [!kith|antagonist] **[[Characters/Suerre Characters/Captain Lucien Moreau\|Captain Lucien Moreau]]** _The Uncatchable Criminal_

Capone considers Moreau the only lawman in Suerré worthy of genuine respect. While the Captain has dismantled numerous Ratpack operations, he has never gathered sufficient evidence to connect them directly to their elusive leader. Their conflict has become a decades-long contest of patience, deception, and careful planning.

> [!kith|antagonist] **[[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Jacque D'aniels\|Jacque D'aniels]]** _Friendly Competition_

Although both men profit from Suerré's criminal underworld, their methods differ considerably. Jacque prefers charm, influence, and negotiation, whereas Capone believes every alliance should ultimately serve his own interests. They occasionally cooperate when profitable but remain careful never to become dependent upon one another.

> [!kith|family] **[[Factions/Suereé Factions/The Ratpack\|The Ratpack]]** _Absolute Loyalty_

The Ratpack's inner circle knows Capone's true nature as a wererat and guards the secret with unwavering devotion. Membership is rewarded generously, but betrayal is answered swiftly. Within the syndicate it is commonly said that "rats always know where the crumbs came from."

#### Personality

Rat Capone is patient, calculating, and unfailingly courteous. He rarely raises his voice, preferring quiet conversations over open threats, yet every sentence carries the implication that he already knows more than those around him.

He views loyalty as a commodity earned through opportunity rather than fear alone. Employees who perform well are rewarded with wealth, protection, and influence, while incompetence is quietly removed before it becomes contagious. Violence, in Capone's view, is simply bad business unless it generates greater profit.

Capone possesses an unusually dry sense of humour and delights in subtle wordplay involving rats, cheese, or traps, often making jokes moments before orchestrating someone's downfall. Despite his criminal reputation, he honours negotiated agreements with surprising consistency, provided the other party fulfils their side first.

His greatest strength is patience. Capone is willing to spend years cultivating a rival's trust before ensuring their inevitable collapse through sabotage rather than assassination.

#### Renown

Among Suerré's criminal underworld, Rat Capone is regarded less as a gang leader and more as a force of nature. Smugglers measure the value of contraband according to whether the Ratpack is willing to transport it, while mercenary companies often seek his approval before accepting contracts within the city.

The existence of the Ratpack's leader has never been officially proven by [[Factions/Suereé Factions/La Garde Ombreuse\|La Garde Ombreuse]], leading many respectable citizens to dismiss him as an exaggerated legend. Criminals, however, know better. It is commonly whispered that every warehouse, dock, tavern cellar, and sewer grate in Suerré hides at least one pair of ears listening on Capone's behalf.

Within the underworld, one saying is repeated with absolute sincerity:

> [!Quote |mark] 
> "Never buy a sword from Rat Capone unless he wants you to win."

#### Trivia

- Rat Capone insists every meeting begins with food, claiming hungry people negotiate poorly.
- His personal cane conceals a finely crafted rapier made from silver-resistant enchanted steel.
- He keeps hundreds of trained sewer rats throughout Suerré, using them to carry messages between Ratpack safehouses.
- Very few people have seen him transform into his wererat form and survived to describe it.
- He despises cats, referring to them only as "tax collectors with whiskers."
- Despite controlling the city's largest criminal organisation, he has never been formally convicted of a single crime.
