**Captain Lucien Moreau** is a senior officer of the [[Factions/Suereé Factions/La Garde Ombreuse\|La Garde Ombreuse]] and commander of the investigation following the murder at the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]]. Known throughout [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]] for his relentless pursuit of criminals and unwavering belief in order above sentiment, Moreau serves as both an obstacle and reluctant ally during the events of [[Quests/Suereé Quests/A Song for the Guilty\|A Song for the Guilty]].

Though feared as **the Hound of Suerré**, his reputation stems less from cruelty than from his refusal to abandon a case once he has taken it.

#### Biography

Born in one of Suerré's poorer canal districts, Lucien Moreau entered the city watch at a young age after witnessing the murder of his father during a gang dispute that was never officially solved. The investigation collapsed amid bribery, political interference, and missing evidence, instilling in Moreau a lifelong distrust of both the city's elite and its justice system.

Determined to become the sort of officer his father never had, Moreau quickly earned recognition for exposing corruption within the constabulary itself. His uncompromising methods won him few friends among politicians, but his extraordinary success in dismantling smuggling rings, extortion rackets, and organised crime secured his promotion to Captain of the [[Factions/Suereé Factions/La Garde Ombreuse\|La Garde Ombreuse]].

Over time, Moreau developed a reputation for solving cases others considered impossible. Criminals learned that fleeing the city rarely mattered, while honest citizens found his investigations almost unnervingly thorough. Despite this, many influential figures view him as dangerous, believing his persistence threatens carefully maintained secrets.

During the events of [[Quests/Suereé Quests/Where the Nightingale Sings\|Where the Nightingale Sings]], Moreau is among the first officers to arrive at the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]] following the murder of a prominent nobleman. Faced with a locked theatre, panicked witnesses, and unfamiliar adventurers seen meeting privately with [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]], he considers the party prime suspects.

As the investigation unfolds throughout [[Quests/Suereé Quests/A Song for the Guilty\|A Song for the Guilty]], Moreau gradually realises the evidence has been manipulated with remarkable precision. Though unwilling to openly trust the party, he quietly allows them enough freedom to pursue leads he cannot investigate through official channels, suspecting powerful individuals within the city are obstructing his own officers.

#### Relationships

> [!kith|friend] **[[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]** _Mutual Distrust_

Moreau and the Nightingale maintain an uneasy relationship. He is fully aware she possesses information capable of exposing many of Suerré's influential figures, yet her refusal to cooperate with official investigations frequently places them at odds. Neither entirely trusts the other, though both recognise they seek the truth through different means.

> [!kith|antagonist] **[[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Jacque D'aniels\|Jacque D'aniels]]** _Persistent Suspect_

Moreau has spent years attempting to uncover the extent of Jacque D'aniels' influence within Suerré's criminal underworld. Although convinced Jacque is connected to numerous illicit operations, he has never obtained evidence sufficient to bring formal charges.

#### Personality

Lucien Moreau is disciplined, perceptive, and relentlessly methodical. He approaches every investigation with clinical precision, preferring physical evidence over testimony and reason over emotion. His calm demeanour often masks an intense determination that borders on obsession whenever confronted with injustice.

Unlike many officers under his command, Moreau cannot be bribed or intimidated, a quality that has earned him admiration from ordinary citizens and resentment from the city's aristocracy. He expects complete honesty from those assisting his investigations and quickly notices inconsistencies in even the smallest details.

Beneath his stern exterior lies a man deeply frustrated by Suerré's political corruption. While he believes firmly in the rule of law, he has become increasingly aware that the law often serves those wealthy enough to manipulate it. This growing disillusionment makes him one of the few authority figures willing to question the official narrative surrounding the theatre murder.

#### Renown

Captain Moreau is widely regarded as one of the most capable investigators in [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. Criminal organisations routinely alter their operations upon learning he has been assigned to a case, while younger constables study his reports as examples of exemplary detective work.

His nickname, **"The Hound of Suerré,"** reflects his relentless pursuit of suspects rather than any unnecessary brutality. Among officers, it is commonly said that once Moreau begins investigating someone, the only question is how long they can remain hidden.

#### Trivia

- Moreau carries a leather notebook containing observations from every major investigation of his career.
    
- He never removes his gloves while examining a crime scene, believing evidence deserves greater care than appearances.
    
- He is an accomplished chess player and frequently compares criminal investigations to unfinished games.
    
- Despite serving the city for decades, he has never attended one of the Nightingale's performances as a guest.
    
- His office contains hundreds of unsolved case files, each meticulously organised and revisited every year.
