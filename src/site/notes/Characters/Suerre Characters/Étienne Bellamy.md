Étienne Bellamy is an amateur detective in [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]] known for his habit of investigating people and events that are, more often than not, entirely innocent. He believes himself to possess a keen investigative mind, although his conclusions frequently rely on little more than coincidence and speculation.

>[!Quote |mark] [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Étienne Bellamy\|Étienne Bellamy]]
>"Any one of these things might be innocent. All six? Impossible."

#### Biography

Étienne's background is largely unknown. At some point, he developed an interest in detective work and began conducting his own investigations throughout Suerré.

His most notable investigation concerned [[Characters/Suerre Characters/Marcel Fournier\|Marcel Fournier]], a local baker whom Étienne became convinced was involved in a criminal conspiracy. Étienne spent five nights watching Marcel's bakery from a nearby rooftop, documenting a series of entirely ordinary activities as suspicious evidence.

The investigation eventually revealed nothing more sinister than a baker buying flour, speaking to customers and closing his shop at the same time each evening.

#### Relationships

>[!kith|antagonist] **[[Characters/Suerre Characters/Marcel Fournier\|Marcel Fournier]]** _Suspect_

Marcel became the subject of Étienne's investigation after Étienne noticed several supposedly suspicious patterns surrounding his bakery. Marcel considers Étienne's surveillance an ongoing nuisance.

#### Personality

Étienne is observant, enthusiastic and completely convinced of his own abilities. He possesses a genuine desire to uncover wrongdoing, but his eagerness to find a mystery often leads him to create one where none exists. He has a tendency to interpret ordinary events as evidence of elaborate conspiracies and is remarkably resistant to explanations that contradict his theories.

Despite this, Étienne is not malicious. He genuinely believes he is helping Suerré by uncovering crime and is capable of accepting that he was wrong, provided someone can demonstrate it convincingly.

#### Renown

Étienne has little real reputation as a detective, although a small number of Suerré's residents recognise him as the man who can frequently be found watching people from rooftops.
