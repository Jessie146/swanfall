**Stumbly John** is a minor yet locally well-known figure in the region of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]], recognized primarily for his chronic drunkenness and rambling anecdotes about a life that might have been. Once an aspiring recruit to the esteemed [[Factions/Silverleaf Woods Factions/Keralan Knights\|Keralan Knights]], **John** failed to pass the Order’s entrance trials, an event widely believed to have marked the turning point in his life. In the years since, he has become a permanent fixture of the region's taverns, particularly [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/The Gilded Griffin Inn\|The Gilded Griffin Inn]], where he spends most evenings recounting exaggerated tales of heroism.

#### Biography

Little is recorded of **John’s** early years, though it is widely believed he was born and raised within [[Locations/Primary Locations/Keria\|Keria]] or its surrounding countryside. As a youth, he reportedly displayed a strong physical constitution and an earnest desire to rise above his modest origins. Like many young men of the region, **John** aspired to join the prestigious [[Factions/Silverleaf Woods Factions/Keralan Knights\|Keralan Knights]], the region’s primary military order.

**John’s** defining moment came during the Aspirant Trials held at the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/Keria Tournament Grounds\|Keria Tournament Grounds]]. These trials are known for their rigour, testing not only physical strength and combat ability but also discipline, resolve, and clarity of mind. Accounts of **John’s** performance vary. Some claim he showed early promise but faltered under pressure; others insist he failed spectacularly in one of the later challenges. A persistent local rumour suggests that a single critical mistake, often embellished in retellings, led to his disqualification. Regardless of the exact circumstances, John did not pass the trials, an outcome that reportedly devastated him.

Following his failure, John withdrew from public life for a time before re-emerging as a markedly changed individual. He developed a reputation for heavy drinking and erratic behavior, earning the nickname [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Stumbly John\|Stumbly John]] due to his unsteady gait and frequent intoxication.

Over the years, he has become a fixture of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Keria\|Keria]]'s streets, known to wander aimlessly or recount exaggerated tales of what “might have been” had he succeeded in the trials. While some townsfolk view him with pity, others regard him as a cautionary tale about the dangers of misplaced ambition and lack of resilience.

#### Personality

Despite his current state, John is not universally disliked. When sober, or at least less intoxicated, he is described as talkative, self-deprecating, and occasionally insightful. He often speaks of honour, duty, and the life he nearly had, suggesting that remnants of his former ambitions still linger beneath his outwardly dishevelled persona.

#### Renown

[[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Stumbly John\|Stumbly John]] occupies a peculiar place in [[Locations/Primary Locations/Keria\|Kerian]] culture. To some, he is a figure of ridicule; to others, a tragic symbol of potential squandered. His story is frequently told to young aspirants as both a warning and a reminder of the trials’ unforgiving nature. 

Whether viewed with scorn, sympathy, or reluctant fondness, Stumbly John remains one of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Keria\|Keria]]’s most recognizable and enduring characters.

#### Trivia
- Despite his reputation, John has never been barred from [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/The Gilded Griffin Inn\|The Gilded Griffin Inn]], reportedly due to an old favour he once did for the inn’s owner, [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Madam Seraphina Blackfeather\|Madam Seraphina Blackfeather]].
- He still wears a faded fragment of his aspirant sash, which he insists is “proof he nearly made it.”
- John is known to loudly critique the techniques of active [[Factions/Silverleaf Woods Factions/Keralan Knights\|Keralan Knights]], often demonstrating moves with surprising (if unsteady) accuracy.
- [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Frosty Wingtip\|Frosty Wingtip]] is rumoured to water down **John’s** drinks when he isn’t looking, though she has neither confirmed nor denied this.
- Children in [[Locations/Primary Locations/Keria\|Keria]] sometimes follow him through the streets, mimicking his exaggerated stumbling, much to the annoyance of their parents.
