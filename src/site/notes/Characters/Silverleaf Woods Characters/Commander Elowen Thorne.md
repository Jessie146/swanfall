**Elowen Thorne**, commonly known as _the Iron Briar_, is a high-standing figure in the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/Thornwatch Frontier\|Thornwatch Frontier]], serving as the commanding officer of the fortified outpost at the edge of the [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]]. She holds the title of **High Warden of [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/Thornwatch Frontier\|Thornwatch Frontier]]** and is widely regarded as a pragmatic and formidable leader tasked with maintaining the fragile boundary between civilization and the encroaching wilds.

#### Biography

Elowen Thorne is a half-elf ranger-general who rose to prominence after surviving a catastrophic expedition into the [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]], in which her entire unit was lost. Her return, alone and changed, cemented her reputation as both a survivor and a figure of quiet mystery. Following the death of her predecessor during a forest incursion, she was appointed High Warden due to her unmatched knowledge of the region and its dangers. Elowen Thorne was not born to command, she earned it. 

Elowen Thorne is known by various titles depending on the speaker:

- **Formal Title:** High Warden of Thornwatch Frontier
- **Common Titles:** The Warden, Briar Warden
- **Ranger Epithets:** The Iron Briar, The Last Pathfinder
- **Mercenary Nicknames:** Steel-Eyes Thorne, The Gatekeeper
- **Rumoured Names:** The Briar Witch, The Seen-and-Returned

She represents the archetype of the frontier warden, an individual shaped by isolation, responsibility, and proximity to the unknown. Her presence at [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/Thornwatch Frontier\|Thornwatch Frontier]] is considered essential to its continued survival, particularly as the threats from the [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]] grow more frequent and less understood. Under her leadership, the Frontier has transformed into a highly disciplined defensive outpost, balancing the competing interests of the [[Factions/Silverleaf Woods Factions/Thornwatch Rangers\|Thornwatch Rangers]], mercenaries, and caravan traders while facing increasing threats from the surrounding forest.

#### Personality

Thorne is characterized by her calm demeanour, sharp observational skills, and reserved nature. She is known to speak little, preferring decisive action over rhetoric. Her leadership style is highly structured, emphasizing constant vigilance, strict patrol rotations, and controlled access to Thornwatch.  Being highly pragmatic, Elowen values survival over glory.

Though often perceived as cold or distant, she is deeply protective of those under her command. She maintains a careful balance between the disciplined Thornwatch Rangers, unpredictable mercenaries, and vulnerable traders who rely on the outpost for safe passage.
