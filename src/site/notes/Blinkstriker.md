The **Blinkstriker** is a [[Class/Riftblade\|Riftblade]] who has mastered the art of instantaneous movement. Rather than using large rifts to cross the battlefield, **Blinkstrikers** create tiny tears in space that allow them to blink from one tile to another between attacks.

A **Blinkstriker** rarely fights from the same position twice. Their greatest strength is the ability to reach vulnerable targets, deliver several attacks in rapid succession, and disappear before the enemy can retaliate.

#### Blinkstriker Mechanics

##### Blink

Blinkstriker abilities generate **Blink Charges**.

Blink Charges allow the Blinkstriker to teleport short distances without consuming their normal movement. Certain attacks generate additional charges when they hit an enemy, allowing the Blinkstriker to chain together attacks and teleports.

##### Momentum

The Blinkstriker gains **Momentum** when they teleport before attacking.

Momentum can increase damage, critical chance, or the range of subsequent Blinkstriker abilities. Remaining stationary causes Momentum to decay.

#### Signature Abilities

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Riftblade Abilities/Blink Strike\|Blink Strike]]**

Teleport to an enemy within range and immediately attack them. If the Blinkstriker has Momentum, the attack deals additional damage.

**[[Flashstep\|Flashstep]]**

Teleport to an unoccupied tile within range. Flashstep does not trigger opportunity attacks.

**[[Execution Rift\|Execution Rift]]**

Teleport behind a wounded enemy and perform a powerful dimensional strike. If the attack kills the target, the Blinkstriker may immediately Blink again.

**[[Aftercut\|Aftercut]]**

Leave a dimensional afterimage at the starting tile. After a short delay, the afterimage repeats the Blinkstriker's most recent attack.

**[[Death Between Steps\|Death Between Steps]]**

The Blinkstriker rapidly teleports between several enemies, striking each one before disappearing to a final tile. The ability deals additional damage for every successful teleport.
