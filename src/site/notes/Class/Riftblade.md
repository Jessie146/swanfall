The **Riftblade** is a martial combatant who has learned to manipulate the fabric of space itself. Armed with a sword and relying on speed, precision, and dimensional magic, **Riftblades** tear open temporary rifts to move across the battlefield and strike enemies from impossible angles.

Rather than meeting an enemy head-on, a **Riftblade** can disappear from one tile and emerge from another in the same motion. Their sword can briefly cut through the boundary between dimensions, allowing attacks to bypass conventional armour and defenses.

In combat, the Riftblade excels at **mobility and single-target damage**. They teleport between strikes, appear beside vulnerable enemies, and leave behind dimensional afterimages that repeat their attacks moments after the original blow.

> [!Quote |mark]  
> “You cannot defend against a blade that was never there.”

#### Role and Playstyle

**Primary Role:** Melee DPS  
**Combat Style:** Sword / Mobile Melee  
**Damage Types:** Physical, Shadow, Void  
**Preferred Range:** Close  
**Difficulty:** High

The **Riftblade** is a highly mobile melee damage dealer designed around movement, positioning, and rapid attacks. Rather than remaining in one position, the Riftblade constantly moves around the battlefield, using rifts to reach enemies that would otherwise be difficult to access.

The **Riftblade** performs best when attacking isolated or vulnerable targets. Their ability to teleport across the grid allows them to bypass frontline enemies, reach enemy spellcasters, and escape before retaliation. The class rewards players who carefully plan their movement. A Riftblade that simply charges into the centre of an enemy formation may quickly find themselves surrounded, while one that chains their abilities together can strike several times without ever remaining in the same place.

#### Combat Identity and Themes

The **Riftblade** is designed around **mobility, dimensional damage, and repeated attacks**.

On the tile grid, the **Riftblade** treats distance differently from other melee classes. A normal warrior must move from one tile to another, while a **Riftblade** can simply tear open a rift and appear beside their target.

Their attacks can also leave behind **Afterimages**, temporary echoes of the **Riftblade** that repeat previous attacks or strike enemies independently.

- **Dimensional Assault** — Teleport directly into melee range.
- **Rift Cutting** — Slice through dimensions to bypass armour.
- **Afterimages** — Leave echoes behind that repeat attacks.
- **Hit and Vanish** — Strike an enemy before teleporting away.
- **Target Isolation** — Focus overwhelming damage on a single enemy.
- **Battlefield Mobility** — Ignore conventional movement restrictions.

#### Class Mechanics

##### Rifts

**Rifts** are temporary tears in space created by the **Riftblade**. A **Riftblade** can use rifts to teleport between tiles, allowing them to cross distances without following the normal movement rules of the battlefield. Rifts may be created by certain abilities and remain on the battlefield for a limited number of turns.

Depending on the ability used, a Rift may:

- Allow the Riftblade to teleport to its location.
- Cause damage when the Riftblade passes through it.
- Trigger an additional attack when entered.
- Allow the Riftblade to teleport behind an enemy.
- Connect two distant points on the battlefield.
- Be consumed to empower another ability.

##### Dimensional Cuts

The Riftblade's sword can temporarily pass through the boundary between dimensions. **Dimensional Cuts** partially or completely bypass conventional physical protection, allowing the Riftblade to damage enemies regardless of their [[System/Physical Armour\|Physical Armour]]. These attacks are particularly effective against heavily armoured opponents.

However, dimensional attacks require greater precision than conventional sword strikes and are generally most effective against a single target.

##### Afterimages

Certain Riftblade abilities create **Afterimages** when the Riftblade attacks or teleports. An Afterimage is a temporary dimensional echo of the Riftblade. It may remain on its original tile and repeat an attack after a delay, allowing a single sword strike to become multiple attacks from different positions.

Afterimages can:

- Repeat the Riftblade's previous attack.
- Strike enemies adjacent to their position.
- Remain on the battlefield for several turns.
- Trigger when an enemy enters their tile.
- Disappear after performing an attack.
- Be used as temporary teleportation points.

#### Abilities

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Riftblade Abilities/Riftstep\|Riftstep]]**

**Range:** 5 Tiles  
**Damage:** None

Tear open a rift and teleport to a selected tile. The **Riftblade** may teleport to an unoccupied tile within range without needing to travel through the intervening tiles. Using **Riftstep** immediately before a melee attack may empower that attack.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Riftblade Abilities/Dimensional Slash\|Dimensional Slash]]**

**Range:** 1 Tile  
**Damage:** Physical + Rift

Slice through the target and briefly cut through the space surrounding them. A portion of the attack bypasses the target's [[System/Physical Armour\|Physical Armour]]. If the attack kills the target, the **Riftblade** creates a temporary rift on the target's tile.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Riftblade Abilities/Echo Strike\|Echo Strike]]**

**Range:** 1 Tile  
**Damage:** Physical

Strike an adjacent enemy before creating an **Afterimage** on the **Riftblade**'s previous tile. At the beginning of the **Riftblade**'s next turn, the Afterimage repeats the attack against the same target if they remain within range.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Riftblade Abilities/Rift Assault\|Rift Assault]]**

**Range:** 4 Tiles  
**Damage:** Physical + Rift

Teleport to a selected enemy and immediately perform a sword attack. The **Riftblade** then creates an Afterimage on their starting tile. The Afterimage performs a weaker version of the attack at the beginning of the next turn.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Riftblade Abilities/Sever Space\|Sever Space]]**

**Range:** 3 Tiles  
**Area:** Line

The Riftblade swings their sword through a tear in space, creating a dimensional slash along a line of tiles. Enemies struck by the attack take Rift damage that partially ignores Physical Armour.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Riftblade Abilities/Vanishing Blade\|Vanishing Blade]]**

**Range:** 1 Tile  
**Damage:** Physical

Strike an adjacent enemy before immediately teleporting to an unoccupied tile within 3 tiles. The attack creates an Afterimage at the original location. This ability allows the **Riftblade** to deal damage without remaining within the enemy's immediate reach.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Riftblade Abilities/Hundredfold Rift\|Hundredfold Rift]]**

**Range:** 5 Tiles  
**Damage:** Physical + Rift

The **Riftblade** rapidly tears through space, appearing around the battlefield in a sequence of instantaneous strikes. The **Riftblade** may teleport between several selected tiles, attacking enemies encountered along the path. Each strike creates a brief Afterimage, causing previous attacks to echo across the battlefield. The final strike deals additional Rift damage and ignores a significant portion of the target's [[ Physical Armour\| Physical Armour]].

#### Riftblade Subclasses

At a certain point in their training, **Riftblades** begin to specialise in different applications of dimensional magic. These specialisations are known as **Riftblade Disciplines**.

While all **Riftblades** possess the ability to manipulate space, each Discipline approaches the Rift differently. Some use it to become impossibly fast, others to dominate a single opponent, while others create dimensional echoes of themselves to fight alongside them.

| Subclass          | Core Fantasy                 | Primary Mechanic        | Purpose               |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------- |
| **Blinkstriker**  | _You can't catch me_         | Blink / Momentum        | Burst & assassination |
| **Phase Duelist** | _You and me, nowhere to run_ | Phase Mark / Duel Space | Elite enemies         |
| **Echo Knight**   | _There are five of me_       | Echoes                  | Multi-position combat |

##### [[Blinkstriker\|Blinkstriker]]

**Role:** Assassin / Mobility DPS  
**Combat Style:** Rapid movement and burst damage

Blinkstrikers use short-range rifts to move between attacks at extraordinary speed. They appear beside an enemy, strike, and vanish before their opponent can react.

Blinkstrikers excel at **isolating vulnerable targets, crossing the battlefield, and delivering sudden bursts of damage**.

**Key Themes:**

- Extreme mobility
- Burst damage
- Teleportation
- Target assassination
- Hit-and-run combat

> _“You saw me coming. That was your mistake.”_

##### [[Phase Duellist\|Phase Duellist]]

**Role:** Single-Target DPS / Duelist  
**Combat Style:** One-on-one combat

Phase Duelists specialise in manipulating the space immediately surrounding a single opponent. Rather than constantly moving across the battlefield, they create a private arena of shifting dimensions where distance, positioning, and conventional defence become unreliable.

Phase Duelists excel at **fighting powerful individual enemies**, becoming increasingly dangerous when they can focus their attention on one target.

**Key Themes:**

- One-on-one combat
- Armour penetration
- Counterattacks
- Target isolation
- Sustained single-target damage

> _“There is nowhere to run when I own the space between us.”_

##### [[Echo Knight\|Echo Knight]]

**Role:** Melee DPS / Battlefield Control  
**Combat Style:** Dimensional clones and repeated attacks

Echo Knights manipulate the remnants left behind whenever they move through a rift. These remnants can be given temporary form, creating **dimensional echoes** capable of fighting alongside their creator.

Echo Knights excel at **occupying multiple tiles, surrounding enemies, and repeating attacks from several positions simultaneously**.

**Key Themes:**

- Dimensional clones
- Afterimages
- Multi-target pressure
- Battlefield control
- Repeated attacks

> _“One blade is enough. Two is better. Ten is excessive.”_
