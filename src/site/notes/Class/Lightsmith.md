The **Lightsmith** is a martial wielder of radiant flame, combining disciplined swordsmanship with magic drawn from light, heat, and divine fire. Armed with a **sword and shield**, Lightsmiths stand at the front of battle, protecting their allies while burning away those who threaten them.

Unlike traditional holy warriors, a Lightsmith's power does not necessarily come from a god. Their magic is forged, shaped, and controlled like steel at a smith's anvil, turning light into weapons, barriers, and devastating bursts of flame.

In combat, the Lightsmith excels at controlling space on the battlefield. Their abilities allow them to hold key locations, protect nearby allies, punish enemies that advance too close, and project damaging light and fire across the battlefield.

#### Role and Playstyle

**Primary Role:** Frontline Defender / Magical Damage Dealer  
**Combat Style:** Sword and Shield  
**Damage Types:** Light, Fire, Radiance, Physical  
**Preferred Range:** Close to Medium  
**Difficulty:** Moderate

The **Lightsmith** occupies the space between a heavily armoured knight and an offensive spellcaster. While capable of dealing significant magical damage, their greatest strength lies in their ability to control the battlefield and create safe spaces for their allies.

The **Lightsmith** performs best when fighting near the centre of the conflict. Unlike a purely defensive tank, the **Lightsmith** is encouraged to actively advance and claim territory. By creating Illuminated Ground, protecting allies, and forcing enemies away from key positions, they can gradually establish control over the battlefield.

The **Lightsmith** is ideal for players who enjoy controlling the shape of a battle. Rather than simply dealing the highest possible damage, the **Lightsmith** decides **where the battle is fought**. A well-positioned **Lightsmith** can block a narrow passage, protect vulnerable allies, force enemies into hazardous tiles, and gradually build toward powerful bursts of radiant fire.

#### Combat Identity and Themes

The **Lightsmith** is designed around positioning and area control.

On the tile grid, a **Lightsmith** can create areas of intense light or flame that alter how enemies move around the battlefield. Enemies may be forced to choose between moving through dangerous tiles or taking a longer route, while allies can benefit from protective zones created by the **Lightsmith**. A **Lightsmith** is particularly effective when positioned between their allies and the enemy, using their shield and magical abilities to establish a defensive line.

- **Hold the Line** — Protect allies and control important tiles.
- **Radiant Warfare** — Use light-based magic to damage and weaken enemies.
- **Sacred Flame** — Burn enemies and create hazardous areas.
- **Shielding** — Block attacks and protect nearby allies.
- **Zone Control** — Create beneficial and dangerous areas across the grid.
- **Positioning** — Become stronger when controlling chokepoints and defending allies.

#### Class Mechanics

##### Light

**Lightsmith** abilities generate and manipulate **Light**. **Light** may function as a class-specific combat resource, allowing the **Lightsmith** to build power through defensive actions, successful attacks, or standing within areas illuminated by their own magic. At higher levels, accumulated Light can be spent to empower abilities or unleash powerful finishing attacks.

- Blocking an attack generates **Light**.
- Striking an enemy with a sword generates **Light**.
- Standing in illuminated ground enhances certain abilities.
- Spending **Light** empowers attacks, shields, or areas of effect.

##### Illuminated Ground

Several **Lightsmith** abilities create **Illuminated Ground**. These spaces remain on the battlefield for a number of turns and can interact with other **Lightsmith** abilities.

Depending on the ability used, **Illuminated Ground** may:

- Damage enemies.
- Apply **[[Status Effects/Burning\|Burning]]**.
- Increase ally defences.
- Improve movement or resistance.
- Generate additional Light.
- Reveal invisible or hidden creatures.
- Cause other **Lightsmith** abilities to gain additional effects.

#### Abilities

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Lightsmith Abilities/Radiant Slash\|Radiant Slash]]**

**Range:** 1 Tile  
**Damage:** Physical + Light

Strike an adjacent enemy with an empowered sword attack. If the target is standing on **Illuminated Ground**, the attack deals additional Light damage.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Lightsmith Abilities/Shield of the First Dawn\|Shield of the First Dawn]]**

**Range:** Self / Adjacent Allies
**Duration**: 1 Turn

Raise your shield and create a protective barrier. The **Lightsmith** and adjacent allies gain additional protection until the beginning of the next turn. Blocking an attack while this ability is active generates **Light**.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Lightsmith Abilities/Sunfire\|Sunfire]]**

**Range:** 4 Tiles  
**Area:** 1 Tile

Call down a burst of concentrated flame onto a target tile. Enemies standing on the tile take Fire damage and may become [[Status Effects/Burning\|Burning]]. The targeted tile becomes **Illuminated Ground** for three turns.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Lightsmith Abilities/Blinding Advance\|Blinding Advance]]**

**Range:** 3 Tiles

Move to a selected tile in a burst of light. Enemies adjacent to the destination tile take Light damage and may suffer a temporary accuracy or movement penalty through [[Status Effects/Blinded\|Blinded]].

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Lightsmith Abilities/Forge the Line\|Forge the Line]]**

**Range:** 3 Tiles

Create a line of **Illuminated Ground** across the battlefield. Allies standing on these tiles gain a defensive bonus, while enemies entering them take Light or Fire damage.

**[[Magic/Spell Library/Class Abilities/Lightsmith Abilities/Solar Aegis\|Solar Aegis]]**

**Range:** Self

The **Lightsmith** surrounds themselves with a powerful shield of radiant energy. For several turns, nearby enemies that attack the **Lightsmith** take Light damage. Each successful block increases the strength of the next offensive Light ability.
