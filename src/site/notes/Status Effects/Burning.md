#### Mechanics

>- **Damage:** X-Y Fire Damage per turn.
>- **Armour:** Damage is applied to [[System/Magic Armour\|Magic Armour]] first.
>- **Duration:** 2 turns, unless otherwise specified.
>- **Removal:** Ends when its duration expires or is removed by an appropriate effect.

#### Description

**Burning** is a negative status effect. Characters afflicted by it will receive X-Y Fire Damage each turn. Fire damage is applied to [[System/Magic Armour\|Magic Armour]] before [[System/Vitality Points\|Vitality Points]], with any remaining damage carrying over to Vitality Points.

**Burning** lasts for a specified number of turns. Applying **Burning** again may refresh its duration or increase its damage, depending on the source.
