#### Mechanics 

>- **Damage:** X-Y [[Piercing Damage\|Piercing Damage]] per turn. 
>- **Armour:** Bypasses [[System/Armour\|Armour]]. 
>- **Duration:** 2 turns, unless otherwise specified. 
>- **Removal:** Ends when its duration expires or is removed by an appropriate effect.

#### Description

**Bleeding** is a negative status effect. Characters afflicted by it will receive X-Y [[Piercing Damage\|Piercing Damage]] each turn. Piercing damage deals damage directly to [[System/Vitality Points\|Vitality Points]] and is not affected by [[System/Armour\|Armour]].

**Bleeding** lasts for a specified number of turns. Applying **Bleeding** again may refresh its duration or increase its damage, depending on the source.
