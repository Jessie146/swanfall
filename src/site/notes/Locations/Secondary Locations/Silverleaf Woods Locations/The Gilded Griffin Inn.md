Tucked away in the trees of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]] lies [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/The Gilded Griffin Inn\|The Gilded Griffin Inn]], known for its warm, golden-hued lighting that beckons travellers near and far. The exterior is marked by a hanging sign featuring a majestic griffin in mid-flight. Inside, the ambiance is cosy yet elegant, with rich mahogany wood accents and soft velvet draperies. A large fireplace dominates one end of the main hall, while the other features a well-stocked bar glittering with bottles of exotic liquors and draughts.
