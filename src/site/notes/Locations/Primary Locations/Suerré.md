# [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]

**Suerré** is the largest canal-city in the western kingdoms, renowned for its lantern-lit boulevards, black-market alchemy, and entrenched political corruption. Often called _The City of Veils_, **Suerré** is governed publicly by the [[Factions/Suereé Factions/Council of Ivory\|Council of Ivory]] while much of its underworld is controlled by competing criminal syndicates and aristocratic houses. The largest of which is [[Factions/Suereé Factions/The Ratpack\|The Ratpack]].
