# [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]]

**Silverleaf Woods** is a vast, ancient forest known for its luminous trees, sentient root systems, and deep connection to primal magic. Once a place of balance between nature and civilization, it is now undergoing a mysterious transformation marked by fading light, hostile wildlife, and shifting terrain. The spread of the [[Umbral Growth\|Umbral Growth]] causes concern for the [[Factions/Silverleaf Woods Factions/Keralan Knights\|Keralan Knights]] and locals alike.

Current State
- Increasing environmental instability
- Spread of “Umbral Growth”
- Conflicting faction influence

Subregions
- Elarion Glade
- Thornwatch Frontier
- Glimmerbark Village
- Moonshade Depths
- Umbral Thicket
- Rootways
- First Grove (hidden)
