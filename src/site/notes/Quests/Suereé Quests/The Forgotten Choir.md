Deep beneath Suerré lies an underground sanctuary.

The inhabitants are descendants of those erased from history by The Chorus.

Here the party learns the truth.

The Nightingale is not simply a singer.

She is the final descendant of the original Bellkeepers—

guardians who once exposed corruption through sacred songs impossible to alter or suppress.

The brass bell carried by the murdered noble is one of three keys needed to awaken the cathedral.

The Chorus has already found another.

Only one remains.
