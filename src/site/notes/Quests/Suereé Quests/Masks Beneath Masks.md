The coded map reveals a secret society meeting beneath an annual masquerade.

The party infiltrates the gathering.

Every attendee wears identical porcelain masks.

Members include:

- nobles
- judges
- generals
- crime bosses
- clergy

The organization is known only as

**The Chorus**

Its members communicate entirely through music.

The party overhears plans to silence the Nightingale forever.

Before escaping, they steal:

**The Black Ledger**

A record of decades of bribery, assassinations, and disappearances.
