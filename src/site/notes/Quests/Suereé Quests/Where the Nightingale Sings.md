**Where the Nightingale Sings** follows the party’s search for the mysterious [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]] through the upper-class entertainment district of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. Their search leads them to the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]], where a private performance ends with a nobleman’s murder and the theatre placed under lockdown.

#### Description

The rain never truly stops in [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. It lingers in the air like cigarette smoke, clings to the cobblestones, and gathers in the narrow canals that split the city into districts of velvet luxury and hidden rot. By the time the party arrives from [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]] carrying the black-waxed invitation entrusted to them by [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Jacque D'aniels\|Jacque D'aniels]], the city is already restless.

Rumours drift through taverns and alleyways alike. Cathedral bells heard ringing despite the towers standing abandoned for decades. More guards in the streets than usual. More masked faces after dark. Yet every conversation eventually circles back to one name.

**The Nightingale.**

#### Expanded Objectives

#### 1) Ask About [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]

The party begins their search in the rain-soaked streets of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. The city is crowded with masked visitors, guarded carriages, and nobles preparing for the upcoming masquerade.

Speak with locals throughout the city to learn more about the woman known as **The Nightingale**. The party may discover that:

- The Nightingale is the stage name of a renowned singer.
- Her performances attract nobles, politicians, and crime lords.
- Almost nobody knows her real name.
- She is scheduled to perform at the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]].
- Some citizens believe she works as an informant.
- Others claim that her songs predict future murders.

#### 2) Gain Entry to the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Velvet Quarter\|Velvet Quarter]]

The party travels to the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Velvet Quarter\|Velvet Quarter]], the centre of Suerré’s nightlife. Jazz spills from smoke-filled cabarets while aristocrats and criminals disappear behind velvet curtains.

The district is guarded by doormen who are selective about who they allow inside. The party may use the [[Quest Items/Sealed Invitation\|Sealed Invitation]] to gain access to establishments that would otherwise be closed to outsiders. Possible approaches include:

- Show the invitation to a doorman.
- Convince the doorman that the party is expected.
- Follow a group of masked nobles through a private entrance.
- Find an alternative route through the surrounding streets.

>- The Party: Present the [[Quest Items/Sealed Invitation\|Sealed Invitation]].
>- The Party: [MOXIE] "We're expected guests. Unless the city's nobility has started refusing its own invitations." (DC 12)
>- The Party: [CHUTZPAH] "If you keep us waiting, we'll assume the invitation was meant for someone more important than you." (DC 13)

On a successful check, the doorman accepts the invitation and allows the party into the district.

> **Doorman:** "Keep the seal visible. People who carry invitations like that are usually worth letting through."

A failed check does not prevent entry, but the party must find another route or speak with someone else.

#### 3) Locate the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]]

Information gathered in the Velvet Quarter leads the party towards the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]], an ornate opera house beside one of Suerré’s oldest canals. Masked carriages arrive beneath the rain while armed guards patrol the entrance. Cloaked figures linger in nearby alleys, watching the theatre and the people entering it.

The party may:

- Ask a local performer for directions.
- Follow the arriving carriages.
- Show the invitation to the theatre guards.
- Investigate the cloaked figures.

> **[CERTAIN JE NE SAIS QUOI]** "Those people aren't waiting for the performance. They're watching who arrives." **(DC 13)**

On a successful check, the party notices that the cloaked figures are observing specific guests rather than the theatre itself.

**Optional Encounter: [[Creatures/Humans/Lysa Quickfingers\|Lysa Quickfingers]]**

Before the party reaches the theatre, [[Creatures/Humans/Lysa Quickfingers\|Lysa Quickfingers]] attempts to steal the [[Quest Items/Sealed Invitation\|Sealed Invitation]].

> **Lysa:** "Careful with that, darling. Things like that have a habit of disappearing in Suerré."

>- The Party: "Keep your hands where we can see them."
>- The Party: [CUT OF YOUR JIB] "You've already tried to take it. Don't make me embarrass you." (DC 12)
>- The Party: [MOXIE] "You don't want the invitation. You want to know who sent it." (DC 13)

Success: Lysa retreats or reveals that someone paid her to obtain the invitation.

Failure: Lysa escapes with it, creating a short recovery objective before the party can meet the Nightingale. Combat with [[Creatures/Humans/Lysa Quickfingers\|Lysa Quickfingers]] is required.


#### 4) Attend the Performance

The party enters the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Suereé Locations/Théâtre de Lune\|Théâtre de Lune]] before the curtains rise. Inside, the theatre is filled with candlelight, polished gold, and uneasy conversation. Nobles sit beside wealthy merchants and notorious criminals, each hiding behind elaborate masks.

The party may notice:

- Several nobles avoiding one another.
- City officials speaking privately with crime bosses.
- A cloaked figure watching the audience from a balcony.
- Guards positioned near every exit.
- Guests reacting uneasily to certain subjects of conversation.

When the performance begins, the theatre falls silent. The Nightingale appears in black silk and silver jewellery, her face concealed behind a delicate feathered mask. Her voice is haunting and sorrowful, carrying through the theatre with remarkable clarity.

Several lines of the song seem deliberately chosen.

> **[CHILDLIKE WONDER]** "Those words don't belong in the song. They're a message." **(DC 12)**

On a successful check, the party recognises that certain lyrics are directed towards specific members of the audience.

> The Nightingale is not simply singing. She is communicating with someone.

> **[CERTAIN JE NE SAIS QUOI]** "Watch the audience. The important part may not be happening on stage." **(DC 13)**

On a successful check, the party notices several nobles reacting uneasily to particular lines in the song.

> A few members of the audience exchange looks whenever the Nightingale mentions bells, drowning, and silence.

> **[CUT OF YOUR JIB]** "The woman in the balcony has been watching us since we arrived." **(DC 12)**

On a successful check, the party notices a cloaked observer seated behind smoked glass above the theatre.

#### 5) Deliver Jacque’s Invitation

After the performance, the party is taken backstage to meet [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]. She is dressed in black silk and wears a feathered mask that conceals most of her face. When she sees the invitation, her expression changes.

> **The Nightingale:** "You came from Jacque."

> **The Party:** "He asked us to deliver this."

> **The Nightingale:** "Did Jacque tell you who else received one?"

The party may ask about:

- Jacque’s connection to the Nightingale.
- The meaning of the bell-and-thorns seal.
- The purpose of the masquerade.
- The coded lyrics.
- The identity of the other invitation holders.

The Nightingale admits that she was expecting the party, but refuses to explain why Jacque sent them.

> **The Nightingale:** "Jacque has a habit of sending people into dangerous places and calling it a favour."

Before she can provide a complete explanation, a scream echoes from the upper balcony.

#### 6) Investigate the Murder at the Theatre

A prominent nobleman has been found dead in a velvet chair, a silver-handled dagger buried beneath his ribs. Blood stains the curtains while masked guests attempt to flee. The theatre gates close, and armed officers begin searching the building. Several witnesses identify the party as outsiders who were seen speaking privately with the Nightingale shortly before the murder.

The party may:

- Remain in the theatre and investigate.
- Question witnesses before the guards separate them.
- Search the murder scene.
- Follow the Nightingale backstage.
- Attempt to escape through the theatre’s service passages.

> **[CHUTZPAH]** "If you want answers, stop pointing weapons at the people who might have them." **(DC 13)**

On a successful check, the party gains a short opportunity to question a witness before the guards intervene.

> **[MOXIE]** "We were invited here. If you arrest us, you'll have to explain why the invitation was genuine." **(DC 14)**

On a successful check, the guards agree to delay arresting the party while the initial investigation takes place.

A failed check does not immediately result in combat, but the guards become more suspicious and restrict the party’s movements. Two failed checks cause a combat encounter with the [[Creatures/Humans/Suerré Constable\|Suerré Constable]] and [[Creatures/Humans/Suerré City Guard\|Suerré City Guard]]

The murder investigation becomes the focus of the next quest, [[Quests/Suereé Quests/A Song for the Guilty\|A Song for the Guilty]].
