The Chorus launches its final assault beneath the cathedral.

As fighting erupts,

the Nightingale climbs alone toward the ancient bell.

She intends to ring it.

Doing so will magically reveal every lie hidden within Suerré.

Every forged document.

Every stolen fortune.

Every political assassination.

Every traitor.

The Bell does not kill.

It reveals truth.

The Chorus would rather destroy the entire cathedral than allow that to happen.

The climax becomes a desperate battle while the cathedral collapses around the party.

Depending on player choices:

### Ending I — The Bell Rings

The Nightingale survives.

Suerré erupts into revolution.

The ruling council falls.

Truth spreads across the city.

The Nightingale disappears before dawn.

Only her song remains.

---

### Ending II — The Silent Song

The Bell rings once.

The Nightingale dies protecting it.

Her sacrifice exposes The Chorus.

A new era begins,

though no one ever again hears the voice that changed the city.

---

### Ending III — A City of Masks

The Bell is destroyed.

The conspiracy survives.

The Nightingale vanishes.

Months later, a young street musician is heard singing a familiar melody.

Someone has inherited the song.
