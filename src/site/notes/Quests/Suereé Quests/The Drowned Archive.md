The final key lies within Suerré's submerged archives beneath the oldest canal.

The archive is flooded.

Half collapsed.

Filled with ancient records.

The party must survive:

- collapsing tunnels
- drowned guardians
- assassins from The Chorus

Within lies the city's true history.

Names erased.

Entire bloodlines executed.

Proof that nearly every ruling family in Suerré owes its power to centuries of murder.

The final Bell Key is recovered.

But...

The Chorus knows.
