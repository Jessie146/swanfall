#### Description

[[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Rowan Thorne\|Rowan Thorne]], once a respected scout of the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/Thornwatch Frontier\|Thornwatch Frontier]], vanished weeks prior after leading an unsanctioned expedition into the deeper regions of the [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]]. Reports from returning patrols speak of altered wildlife behaviour, unnatural quiet, and signs of ritual activity.

Elowen insists her brother may be injured or compromised, but avoids elaborating on the nature of his expedition.

Rowan is revealed to be acting with purpose. He claims to have discovered a truth about the [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]], that the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/Thornwatch Frontier\|Thornwatch Frontier]] is not protecting civilization, but suppressing something ancient and necessary. He challenges the party’s assumptions about Elowen’s leadership and the role of Thornwatch.

#### Objectives

- Speak with [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Commander Elowen Thorne\|Commander Elowen Thorne]]
- Find Rowan’s cloak clasp
- Investigate the Outer Woodlands
- Locate the expedition site
- Follow clues into the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/Silverleaf Rootways\|Silverleaf Rootways]]
- Confront Rowan Thorne

#### Expanded Objectives

**1. Speak with [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Commander Elowen Thorne\|Commander Elowen Thorne]]**

Travel to the encampment at the forest’s edge and report to [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Commander Elowen Thorne\|Commander Elowen Thorne]]. She bears the weight of command, and the worry of a missing sibling.

- Receive [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Rowan Thorne\|Rowan Thorne]]’s last known route and orders for the search.
- Search the camp for Rowan’s cloak clasp; its lingering essence may aid in tracking him.

**2. Investigate the Outer Woodlands**

The forest resists you. Paths twist, and silence feels unnatural.

- Follow broken trails, abandoned campsites, and signs of struggle.
- Be wary—woodland creatures behave strangely, moving with eerie coordination.

**3. Discover the Lost Expedition Site**

What remains of Rowan’s expedition lies scattered and disturbed.

- Locate remnants of the party—torn gear, disturbed earth, and signs of sudden conflict.
- Uncover journals or carvings referencing a “voice beneath the roots.”

**4. Track Rowan into the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/Silverleaf Rootways\|Silverleaf Rootways]]**

The forest deepens into something older… and aware.

- Navigate the warped terrain of the Rootways, where roots coil like veins.
- Notice signs of deliberate passage—Rowan is not lost. He is being led.

**5. Confront Rowan Thorne** _(Quest Climax)_

At the heart of the Rootways, you find him. Changed, but not gone.

- Rowan does not attack. Instead, he speaks; calm, distant, and unsettling.
- Choose how to respond as the truth begins to surface.
