Rowan reveals that Elowen knows more than she admits. The frontier’s purpose is not merely defensive, it is containment. He believes the force within the Silverleaf Woods is being wrongfully suppressed, and that its awakening is inevitable.

He asks the players to:

- Retrieve a sealed dispatch or relic from Thornwatch Outpost, **or**
- Investigate a hidden site tied to Elowen’s past actions.
