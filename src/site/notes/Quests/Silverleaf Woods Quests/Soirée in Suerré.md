**Soirée in Suerré** is a short introductory quest that leads the party from the [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]] to the canal-city of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]. After receiving a mysterious invitation from [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Jacque D'aniels\|Jacque D'aniels]], the party must enter the city and deliver it to a contact known only as [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]].

#### Description

The quest begins inside the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/The Gilded Griffin Inn\|The Gilded Griffin Inn]], an aging roadside tavern deep within the rain-soaked [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]]. Hunters, merchants, and travellers crowd around the fire while the storm outside turns the forest roads into mud.

Among the patrons is [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Jacque D'aniels\|Jacque D'aniels]], a former resident of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]] who now spends more time drinking than discussing his past. After overhearing the party speak about work and opportunity, Jacque approaches them with an unusual proposition.

> **Jacque:** "You look like people who haven't yet learned to mind their own business. That's useful in a city like Suerré."

Jacque produces a sealed invitation embossed with black wax and trimmed in gold foil. The invitation is addressed to a noble household in Suerré and grants entry to an upcoming masquerade soirée. According to Jacque, the hosts are expecting the party to deliver the invitation to a contact known only as [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]. He offers little explanation, insisting that the less the party knows, the safer they will be. The seal bears no noble crest. Instead, it depicts a bell surrounded by thorned vines. The parchment smells faintly of expensive perfume and damp canal water, while several letters appear to have been blurred by rain.

> **Jacque:** "Get that invitation into the right hands, and you'll have yourself a friend in Suerré. Get it into the wrong hands, and you'll have yourself a very short evening."

The party must travel from the [[Locations/Primary Locations/Silverleaf Woods\|Silverleaf Woods]] to [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]], gain entry to the city, and locate [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]. Their arrival begins a chain of events that will eventually draw them into the hidden affairs of the canal-city.

#### Expanded Objectives

##### **1. Speak with [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Jacque D'aniels\|Jacque D'aniels]]**

Find [[Characters/Silverleaf Woods Characters/Jacque D'aniels\|Jacque D'aniels]] inside the [[Locations/Secondary Locations/Silverleaf Woods Locations/The Gilded Griffin Inn\|The Gilded Griffin Inn]]. He can usually be found sitting alone in the corner, turning a coin between his fingers.

The party may ask Jacque about:

- The history of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]].
- The masquerade soirée.
- The identity of [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]].
- The meaning of the bell-and-thorns seal.
- Jacque's own connection to the city.

> **The Party:** "Why are you sending us?"
> 
> **Jacque:** "Because nobody in Suerré knows your faces yet. That's an advantage I haven't had in years."

If questioned about the invitation, Jacque admits that it was intended for someone who cannot safely collect it themselves.

> **Jacque:** "You're not being asked to solve a murder. You're being asked to deliver a letter. Try not to turn it into anything more complicated."

The party receives the [[Quest Items/Sealed Invitation\|Sealed Invitation]].

##### **2. Investigate the [[Quest Items/Sealed Invitation\|Sealed Invitation]]**

The party may inspect the invitation before leaving the inn.

> **[CHILDLIKE WONDER]** "The seal doesn't belong to any noble house. Why would a noble invitation be marked like this?" **(DC 10)**

On a successful check, the party notices that the bell-and-thorns symbol appears to have been pressed into the wax separately from the rest of the seal.

> The invitation was sealed twice. The first seal was broken and replaced with the mark of the bell surrounded by thorns.

> **[CERTAIN JE NE SAIS QUOI]** "This invitation has passed through too many hands." **(DC 12)**

On a successful check, the party recognises that the perfume and canal-water scent may be deliberate rather than accidental.

> The invitation smells as though it has been carried through the city before being sent into the woods.

> **[MOXIE]** "Jacque knows more about this than he's telling us." **(DC 12)**

On a successful check, the party notices Jacque become visibly uncomfortable when the seal is mentioned.

> Jacque's smile fades for half a second.
> 
> Whatever the invitation is, he does not want to discuss it.

A failed check reveals nothing beyond the fact that the invitation is genuine and addressed to a noble household in Suerré.

##### **3. Travel to [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]]**

The journey takes the party along fog-covered trade roads and through isolated settlements on the edge of the forest.

Along the way, the party hears several rumours about the city:

- Noble families are preparing for the annual masquerade.
- Guard patrols have increased around the canals.
- Several bodies have recently been recovered from the water.
- Masked visitors have been arriving by carriage.
- The city is said to be full of spies, smugglers, and assassins.

##### **4. Gain Entry to the Canal-City**

The party reaches the gates of [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]] and discovers that entry is being closely monitored.

The city guard demands to know why the party is entering the city and whether they have business there. The party may gain entry in several ways.

> **[MOXIE]** "We have been invited to the masquerade. Unless the city's nobility has started refusing its own guests." **(DC 12)**

On a successful check, the guard accepts the invitation as proof of legitimate business and allows the party to enter.

> **Suerré City Guard:** "Keep your weapons sheathed and your masks off until you're inside. We don't need another incident at the gates."

> **[CERTAIN JE NE SAIS QUOI]** "The guard isn't checking the invitation. He's checking whether we look like trouble." **(DC 12)**

On a successful check, the party identifies a less heavily guarded entrance used by merchants and deliveries.

The party may enter through the service road without drawing attention.

> **[CUT OF YOUR JIB]** "Wait until the next carriage passes. We'll follow it through the gate." **(DC 13)**

On a successful check, the party slips past the guards by blending into the flow of arriving merchants and servants.

> The guards barely glance at the party as they pass beneath the gatehouse.

**Combat Option**

If the party attacks the [[Creatures/Humans/Suerré City Guard\|Suerré City Guard]], combat begins. The city will become hostile to the party, and future attempts to enter through the main gate will be more difficult.

**Alternative Entry**

The party may also discover a concealed entrance beneath one of the canal bridges.

> **[CERTAIN JE NE SAIS QUOI]** "That wall is too clean. Something has been opening and closing it recently." **(DC 14)**

The hidden entrance leads into a maintenance passage beneath the city. It allows the party to enter Suerré without speaking to the guards, but places them in an unfamiliar part of the city.

**5. Find [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]**

Once the party enters [[Locations/Primary Locations/Suerré\|Suerré]], they must locate [[Characters/Suerre Characters/The Nightingale\|The Nightingale]]. Jacque has provided no address. Instead, he gives the party a single instruction:

> **Jacque:** "When you hear the nightingale sing, follow the sound. If you don't hear it, you're not looking in the right place."

The party may ask around the city, investigate the invitation, or search for references to the bell-and-thorns symbol.

The quest concludes when the party begins searching for the Nightingale. The follow-up quest [[Quests/Suereé Quests/Where the Nightingale Sings\|Where the Nightingale Sings]] is then given.
