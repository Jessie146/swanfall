## **Riftblade**

**Theme:** Melee + space magic  
**Role:** Melee DPS
- Teleports between strikes
- Cuts through dimensions (ignores armor)
- Leaves “afterimage attacks”
**Subclass ideas:**
- Blinkstriker (fast assassin)
- Phase Duelist (1v1 specialist)
- Echo Knight (clone-based combat)

1. Chronist
Theme: Time manipulation
Role: Support / Utility DPS
Rewinds damage (healing through time reversal)
Applies “delayed damage” effects
Can accelerate allies or slow enemies
Subclass ideas:
Timewarden (defensive support)
Paradoxist (high-risk DPS)
Fatecaller (buff/debuff specialist

Archivist
Theme: Storing and replaying events
Records actions (damage, movement, spells)
Replays them later at will
Can “overwrite” current events with past ones
Plays like delayed strategy and memory manipulation

Debt Collector / Arbiter / Tithebearer
Theme: Magical contracts and consequences
Enemies accumulate “debt” (damage owed, actions taken)
Collector “cashes in” for massive effects
Punishes healing, buffs, or ability spam
Combat becomes about inevitability, not burst
