**Elarion Glade**
Overview
A sacred clearing at the heart of Silverleaf Woods where druids commune with the forest. The trees here exhibit heightened awareness and responsiveness.
Inhabitants
- Verdant Circle Druids
- Bound Forest Spirits
- Awakened Guardians
Notable Features
- Ritual Circles (unstable)
- Whispering Trees
- Spirit Convergence Points
Quests
- The Fading Glow
- Roots of Corruption
- Spirits in Discord
- Echo of the First Tree

**First Grove**
Overview
The origin point of Silverleaf Woods and the site of the first binding ritual.
Unlock Conditions
- Discover all major memory fragments
- Complete key faction questlines
Notable Features
- The First Tree
- Binding Circle
- Umbral Heart Chamber

**The Umbral Heart**
Overview
A primordial entity bound beneath Silverleaf Woods. Source of both life and corruption.
Nature
- Not inherently evil
- Embodies primal growth and decay
History
- Bound by ancient druids
- Pact required continuous maintenance
- Current events caused by destabilization
Role in Story
Central figure in the zone-wide conflict and final decision point.

**The Great Pact**
Overview
An ancient agreement between druids and the Umbral Heart to regulate the forest’s power.
Key Elements
- Binding ritual
- Balance cycle
- Shared dependence
Truth (Hidden)
- Pact was not consensual
- The Heart was betrayed
- The forest retains memory of this event

**Main Storyline**
Title
**The Heart Beneath the Leaves**
Summary
Players uncover the truth behind the forest’s corruption, revealing a broken pact and a sentient force seeking resolution.
