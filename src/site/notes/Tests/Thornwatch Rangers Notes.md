## **Thornwatch Rangers – Rank Structure**

The **Thornwatch Rangers** are a semi-military order tasked with defending the frontier boundary of the Silverleaf Woods. Their hierarchy blends traditional command roles with specialized woodland functions, reflecting the unique demands of forest warfare and survival.

---

## **High Command**

### **High Warden**

**Current Holder:** Elowen Thorne  
**Role:** Supreme commander of Thornwatch Frontier  
The High Warden holds absolute authority over all ranger operations, including military defense, scouting, and enforcement of frontier law. The position requires both strategic leadership and deep knowledge of the Silverleaf Woods.

---

### **Warden-Commander**

**Role:** Senior operational commander  
Second only to the High Warden, the Warden-Commander oversees large-scale deployments, coordinates patrol rotations, and relays orders to lower ranks. Often commands in the High Warden’s absence.

---

## **Senior Officers**

### **Wardens**

**Role:** Unit leaders and territory overseers  
Wardens command ranger companies and are responsible for specific sectors of the forest boundary. They interpret orders, manage patrols, and make critical decisions in the field.

---

### **Pathmasters**

**Role:** Masters of navigation and deep-forest knowledge  
Pathmasters are elite rangers who specialize in mapping, navigation, and understanding the shifting terrain of the Silverleaf Woods. Their guidance is essential for any operation beyond the known frontier.

---

## **Field Command**

### **Thorn Captains**

**Role:** Patrol leaders  
Thorn Captains lead smaller units on patrol, escort missions, and skirmishes. They are experienced fighters capable of independent command in dangerous situations.

---

### **Senior Rangers**

**Role:** Veteran operatives  
Highly experienced rangers trusted with complex assignments. Often act as advisors to captains and mentors to lower ranks.

---

## **Core Ranks**

### **Rangers**

**Role:** Standard rank-and-file  
The backbone of Thornwatch. Rangers perform patrols, guard duty, scouting, and combat operations.

---

### **Greenwardens**

**Role:** Specialized environmental watchers  
Greenwardens monitor the health and behavior of the forest itself—tracking unnatural changes, blight, or signs of encroaching threats. They often work closely with Pathmasters.

---

## **Junior Ranks**

### **Initiates**

**Role:** Trainees and probationary members  
New recruits undergoing training in survival, combat, and forest lore. Rarely permitted beyond the inner perimeter without supervision.

---

### **Watchlings**

**Role:** Scouts-in-training / messengers  
Young or newly inducted members assigned to basic duties such as message running, observation, and assisting patrols.

---

## **Special Roles (Non-Linear Positions)**

These roles can be held alongside rank:

- **Nightwardens:** Specialists in nocturnal patrols and threats
- **Beastwardens:** Experts in tracking and handling wildlife
- **Emberkeepers:** Responsible for controlled fire use and signaling
- **Silent Hands:** Covert operatives and spies within or beyond the frontier

---

## **Structure Notes**

- Authority flows strictly downward, but **field autonomy is expected** due to unpredictable forest conditions
- Rangers often defer to **Pathmasters** in unknown terrain, regardless of rank
- The High Warden may override any decision without explanation
- Promotions are based on **survival, judgment, and experience**, not lineage

---

## **Cultural Elements**

- Rangers carve personal marks into their gear to signify survival milestones
- Taking command of a patrol for the first time is considered a major rite of passage
- It is said: _“Rank matters at the gate. In the woods, only instinct keeps you breathing.”_

# **Thornwatch Rangers – Ranks & Trials**

## **1. Watchling**

_“You see—but you do not yet understand.”_

**Role:** Messengers, lookouts, assistants  
Watchlings are not trusted with the woods yet. They stay close to Thornwatch, learning discipline before danger.

### **Trial: The Still Hour**

- The Watchling must stand a full night watch **alone on the outer wall**
- No speaking, no fire, no leaving position
- At dawn, they must report **everything they saw and heard**

**What it tests:** Awareness, patience, fear control  
**Failure:** Missing obvious signs, abandoning post

---

## **2. Initiate**

_“You step beyond the gate—and the woods notice.”_

**Role:** Trainees allowed limited patrol exposure

### **Trial: The First Path**

- Sent just beyond the safe boundary with minimal gear
- Must follow marked trails and return before nightfall
- Along the way, they must identify:
    - Safe vs unsafe flora
    - Signs of territorial creatures
    - One _intentional false trail_ set by Rangers

**Twist:** The forest sometimes _changes_ the path

**What it tests:** Observation, memory, adaptability  
**Failure:** Getting lost, trusting the wrong signs

---

## **3. Ranger**

_“Now the woods may try to kill you.”_

**Role:** Full member of Thornwatch

### **Trial: The Thorn Vigil**

- A small group is sent to hold a remote post for 3 nights
- No reinforcement unless absolutely necessary
- They must:
    - Maintain a perimeter
    - Rotate watch properly
    - Survive _whatever comes_

**Important:** Sometimes nothing happens.  
Sometimes something _does_.

**What it tests:** Teamwork, endurance, leadership under stress  
**Failure:** Panic, breaking formation, poor judgment

---

## **4. Senior Ranger**

_“You are no longer reacting—you are deciding.”_

**Role:** Veterans, mentors, problem-solvers

### **Trial: The Broken Patrol**

- Given command of a “routine patrol”
- Something goes wrong (engineered or real):
    - Missing scout
    - Ambush
    - Conflicting orders
- They must resolve it **without guidance**

**What it tests:** Decision-making, responsibility, sacrifice  
**Failure:** Hesitation, unnecessary losses

---

## **5. Thorn Captain**

_“Others live or die by your word.”_

**Role:** Patrol leaders, small-unit commanders

### **Trial: The Silent Route**

- Must lead a unit through a **dangerous, unmarked section** of forest
- Objective: reach a destination _without being detected_
- No fires, minimal noise, controlled movement

**Hidden element:** Rangers observing them may _simulate threats_

**What it tests:** Leadership, stealth, trust-building  
**Failure:** Detection, disorder, loss of control

---

## **6. Pathmaster**

_“You do not follow paths. You define them.”_

**Role:** Masters of terrain, navigation, and forest behavior

### **Trial: The Shifting Deep**

- Sent into a region known for **changing geography or illusions**
- Must:
    - Map a route
    - Return with something _verifiable_ (object, mark, knowledge)
- No time limit—but most take 3–7 days

**Rumor:** Some paths test more than geography

**What it tests:** Intuition, perception, mental resilience  
**Failure:** Never returning… or returning wrong

---

## **7. Warden**

_“You are responsible for more than lives—you guard the line itself.”_

**Role:** Sector commanders

### **Trial: The Line Holds**

- Given control of a vulnerable section of the frontier
- A simulated or real incursion is triggered
- They must:
    - Deploy forces
    - Protect civilians if present
    - Decide what to sacrifice

**What it tests:** Strategy, moral judgment, command presence  
**Failure:** Losing control of the situation

---

## **8. Warden-Commander**

_“You see the whole board—and what moves in the dark.”_

**Role:** Operational leader of all ranger forces

### **Trial: The Fracture**

- Multiple crises occur at once:
    - Breach in one sector
    - Missing patrol in another
    - Political pressure from traders/mercs
- Must resolve all **without full information**

**What it tests:** Multitasking, prioritization, emotional control  
**Failure:** Choosing wrong priorities

---

## **9. High Warden**

_“You are the boundary.”_

**Role:** Absolute authority

### **Trial: The Unnamed**

There is no formal record of this trial.

But whispers say:

- The candidate must go **alone into the deepest part of Silverleaf**
- They return changed… or not at all
- And if they return, the previous High Warden steps down—or is already gone

**What it tests:** Unknown  
**What it costs:** Also unknown

---

# **Cultural Details That Bring It Alive**

- Rangers don’t celebrate promotions loudly—**they acknowledge survival quietly**
- Each trial leaves a mark:
    - A notch in a blade
    - A carved symbol
    - A scar they don’t explain
- Failing a trial doesn’t always mean death—  
    sometimes it means **you’re never sent out again**

---

# **Tone Lever**

- **Heroic:** Trials are hard but fair, meant to build legends
- **Dark:** Trials are borderline cruel, survival is luck as much as skill
- **Mystical:** The forest itself is part of every trial
- **Horror:** Some trials are not controlled at all

### Within the Rangers:

- Traditionalists vs pragmatists
- Those loyal to Elowen vs those who **fear what she’s becoming**
- Rangers who think Thornwatch should **retreat, not hold**

### Outside pressure:

- Merchants pushing for looser restrictions
- Mercenaries who don’t follow ranger rules
- Distant rulers questioning Elowen’s authority

- Rangers never light open flames beyond the gate
- Every ranger carries a **“last whistle”** (used only when death is certain)
- The gates are covered in **carved warnings from past wardens**
- A rule: _“Never answer a voice from the trees unless you see its face”_

- Rangers age faster (stress, magic, something unnatural)
- Long-term rangers start to **lose memory or identity**
- The forest “marks” those who spend too long inside
- Elowen herself is **paying a price no one else knows**

- A **horn** that calls all rangers back (but hasn’t been used in years)
- A **blade** that reacts to the forest
- A **map that updates itself**
- A **tree inside Thornwatch that should not be there**
