# [[System/Armour\|Armour]]

**Armour** in the realm is protective equipment worn by player characters and NPCs. There are several types of equipment that can be slotted to different parts of the body, including [[Items/Accessories - Items/Accessories\|Accessories]] ([[Items/Accessories - Items/Amulets\|Amulets]] and [[Items/Accessories - Items/Rings\|Rings]]) that grant different effects, stat modifiers, and buffs that will benefit the character. Players can find these pieces of equipment and accessories as part of [[System/Quest\|Quest]] rewards, purchase them from merchants, obtain them from defeated [[Creatures/Creature\|Creatures]], or found in treasure chests.

> #### Contents
> - [[System/Armour#Overview\|#Overview]]

#### Overview

**Armour properties** are split between Physical and Magic armour. Most armour will have one stat better than its counterpart.
- **[[System/Physical Armour\|Physical Armour]]** - Protects against [[System/Physical Damage\|Physical Damage]] (Weapons like Swords, Arrows, Physical Skills and status effects like [[Status Effects/Stunned\|Stunned]])
- **[[System/Magic Armour\|Magic Armour]]** - Protects against [[System/Magic Damage\|Magic Damage]] from different elements like Fire, Ice, and other Magic [[Spell \| Spells]]. Magic Armour can also mitigate status effects caused by magic attacks like [[Status Effects/Burning\|Burning]], [[Status Effects/Frozen\|Frozen]], and [[Status Effects/Charmed\|Charmed]].
