# [[System/Skills\|Skills]]

**Skills** represent the practical abilities and personal talents possessed by characters. While **[[System/Attribute\|Attribute]]** describe a character's innate qualities, Skills determine how those qualities are applied to the world, influencing combat, exploration, dialogue and other interactions.

There are **24 Skills**, divided equally among the six Attributes: [[Attributes/Chutzpah (CHU)\|Chutzpah (CHU)]], [[Attributes/Childlike Wonder (CHI)\|Childlike Wonder (CHI)]], [[Attributes/Cut Of Your Jib (CTJ)\|Cut Of Your Jib (CTJ)]], [[Attributes/Gumption (GMP)\|Gumption (GMP)]], [[Attributes/Certain Je Ne Sais Quoi (JNSQ)\|Certain Je Ne Sais Quoi (JNSQ)]] and [[Attributes/Moxie (MOX)\|Moxie (MOX)]]. A character's proficiency in a Skill is influenced primarily by its associated Attribute, with particularly skilled characters capable of succeeding at difficult tasks that others might find impossible.

> - [[System/Skills#Skill Checks\|#Skill Checks]]

#### Skill Checks

When a character attempts an action where the outcome is uncertain, the appropriate Skill may be tested against a **Difficulty Class (DC)**. The character's relevant Attribute and Skill proficiency are taken into account when determining the result.

Skills may be used both actively and passively. A character might consciously use **Blag** to convince a guard of a fabricated story, while **Hunch** might reveal that something about the guard's behaviour seems suspicious without the player actively searching for it.
