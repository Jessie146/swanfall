## ⚙️ Optional Add-ons

- **Cursed** (variant): Strong but with drawbacks
    
- **Ethereal** (variant): Temporary but very powerful
- **Corrupted** (variant): Building stat that brings greater detrimental effects
    
- **Set Items**: Gain bonuses when combined
