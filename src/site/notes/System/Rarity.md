# [[System/Rarity\|Rarity]]

Each item found within the realm has a **rarity** which indicates the quality or nature of the base modifications available on the item. Higher rarity [[Items/Accessories - Items/Equipment\|Equipment]] items provide larger [[System/Attribute\|Attribute]] and [[System/Armour\|Armour]] bonuses, while higher rarity [[Items/Food and Drink Consumables - Items/Food and Drink Consumables\|Food and Drink Consumables]] provide greater bonuses and [[System/Effects\|Effects]]. The item's name and rarity are colour-coded in the item's tooltip as displayed in the tables below. [[System/Equipment Enhancements\|Equipment Enhancements]] are denoted by an asterisk *.

#### Equipment Rarity
The equipment rarity system reflects both the material quality and narrative significance of items within the realm. At its lower tiers, such as <span style="color:rgb(157, 157, 157)">Damaged</span> and <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 255)">Worn</span>, equipment tells stories of hardship, survival, and decline. <span style="color:rgb(30,255,0)">Standard</span> and <span style="color:rgb(0,112,221)">Refined</span> items represent the functional backbone of society, where craftsmanship meets practicality. At the upper tiers, <span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">Masterwork</span> and <span style="color:rgb(255, 128, 0)">Relic</span> items embody mastery and history, bridging the gap between skill and legend. Finally, <span style="color:rgb(230,204,128)">Ascendant</span> equipment exists beyond conventional hierarchy, serving as mythic artifacts that shape the course of the world itself.
##### <span style="color:rgb(157, 157, 157)">- Damaged</span>
<span style="color:rgb(157, 157, 157)">Damaged</span> equipment represents items that have suffered significant wear, neglect, or catastrophic failure. These pieces are often barely functional, with cracked structures, dulled edges, or unstable enchantments. In many cases, they impose penalties on the user, such as reduced effectiveness or a higher chance of breaking during use. <span style="color:rgb(157, 157, 157)">Damaged</span> items are typically found in ruins, scavenged from battlefields, or carried by the desperate and impoverished.

{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(255,255,255)">- Worn</span>
<span style="color:rgb(255,255,255)">Worn</span> equipment has seen consistent use but remains serviceable. Though no longer in peak condition, these items retain much of their original function. Edges may be slightly dulled, materials weathered, and enchantments faded, yet they are reliable enough for everyday use. <span style="color:rgb(255,255,255)">Worn</span> gear is common among travellers, militia, and those who cannot afford higher-quality craftsmanship.

<span style="color:rgb(255,255,255)">Worn</span> quality weapons and [[System/Armour\|Armour]] usually have no [[System/Attribute\|Attribute]] bonuses.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Worn Equipment
>  - [[Quest Items/Henri's Pocketwatch\|Henri's Pocketwatch]]
> 
{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(30,255,0)">- Standard</span>
<span style="color:rgb(30,255,0)">Standard</span> equipment is the baseline for quality and performance. These items are well-made, properly maintained, and function exactly as intended without bonuses or drawbacks. Produced by competent craftsmen using reliable materials, <span style="color:rgb(30,255,0)">Standard</span> gear is widely available and forms the backbone of most armies and adventurers’ kits. It represents a balance of affordability and dependability.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Standard Equipment
>  - [[Items/Accessories - Items/Meadowheart Amulet\|Meadowheart Amulet]]
> 
{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(0,112,221)">- Refined</span>
<span style="color:rgb(0,112,221)">Refined</span> equipment is crafted with greater care and precision than standard items. Superior materials, improved techniques, or minor enchantments elevate these items above the ordinary. They often provide modest benefits, such as increased durability, sharper edges, or enhanced efficiency. <span style="color:rgb(0,112,221)">Refined</span> gear is favoured by seasoned adventurers and professionals who require dependable tools with an extra edge.
> [!column|dataview 4]+ Refined Equipment
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">- Masterwork</span>
<span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">Masterwork</span> items are the pinnacle of mortal craftsmanship. Forged or constructed by highly skilled artisans, these pieces exhibit exceptional quality, balance, and durability. Every detail is meticulously perfected, often incorporating rare materials or advanced techniques. <span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">Masterwork</span> equipment frequently provides significant advantages and may serve as the foundation for powerful enchantments. Such items are rare and highly sought after.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Masterwork Equipment
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(255,128,0)">- Relic</span>
<span style="color:rgb(255,128,0)">Relic</span> equipment carries historical, cultural, or mystical significance. These items are often ancient, tied to legendary figures, lost civilizations, or pivotal events. Their power may stem from forgotten magic, divine influence, or the accumulation of centuries of use and reverence. <span style="color:rgb(255,128,0)">Relics</span> typically possess unique abilities that cannot be replicated by modern means, making them invaluable and often irreplaceable.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Relic Equipment
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(230,204,128)">- Ascendant</span>
<span style="color:rgb(230,204,128)">Ascendant</span> equipment transcends conventional understanding, existing at the boundary between artifact and myth. These items are imbued with immense, often reality-altering power, and may possess a will or purpose of their own. Their origins are frequently divine, cosmic, or otherwise beyond mortal comprehension. <span style="color:rgb(230,204,128)">Ascendant</span> gear can dramatically alter the fate of individuals, nations, or entire worlds, and is exceedingly rare—sometimes believed to be singular in existence.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Ascendant Equipment
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}

#### Food and Drink Consumable Rarity
The consumable rarity system reflects both the quality and mystical significance of food and drink within the realm. Lower tiers, such as <span style="color:rgb(157, 157, 157)">Rotten</span> and <span style="color:rgb(255,255,255)">Basic</span>, emphasize survival and the harsh realities of scarcity. <span style="color:rgb(30,255,0)">Deluxe</span> and <span style="color:rgb(0,112,221)">Feast</span> tiers highlight culture, prosperity, and the role of food in social and ceremonial life. <span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">Exotic</span> consumables introduce mystery and the influence of distant lands, while <span style="color:rgb(255,128,0)">Mythic</span> and <span style="color:rgb(230,204,128)">Divine</span> tiers elevate nourishment into the realm of legend and transcendence.
##### <span style="color:rgb(157, 157, 157)">- Rotten</span>
<span style="color:rgb(157, 157, 157)">Rotten</span> consumables are spoiled, contaminated, or otherwise unsafe to consume. These items often carry foul odours, discoloration, and harmful effects, ranging from mild sickness to severe poisoning. They are typically found in neglected stores, disaster-stricken regions, or as the result of failed preservation. While desperate individuals may consume them out of necessity, doing so is widely regarded as a dangerous gamble.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Rotten Food and Drink Consumables
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(255,255,255)">- Basic</span>
<span style="color:rgb(255,255,255)">Basic</span> consumables are simple, nourishing, and widely available. These foods and drinks provide essential sustenance without any notable enhancements or drawbacks. Often consisting of staple ingredients prepared with minimal effort, they are the daily fare of common folk, soldiers, and travellers. Their value lies in reliability rather than refinement.
> [!column|dataview 4]+ Basic Food and Drink Consumables
>  - [[Items/Food and Drink Consumables - Items/Stonebrew\|Stonebrew]]
> 
{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(30,255,0)">- Deluxe</span>
<span style="color:rgb(30,255,0)">Deluxe</span> consumables represent a step above the ordinary, prepared with care and higher-quality ingredients. These items are more flavourful, more satisfying, and occasionally offer minor restorative or invigorating effects. Common in prosperous households, inns, and celebrations, deluxe foods are a sign of comfort and modest wealth.

<span style="color:rgb(30,255,0)">Deluxe</span> food and drink generally provides short-duration bonuses or greater restorative properties.
> [!column|dataview 4]+ Deluxe Food and Drink Consumables
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}
##### <span style="color:rgb(0,112,221)">- Feast</span>
<span style="color:rgb(0,112,221)">Feast</span>-tier consumables are elaborate meals or drinks crafted for grand gatherings, ceremonies, or noble courts. They combine exceptional ingredients, skilled preparation, and often subtle magical enhancement. Consuming such fare can grant noticeable benefits, such as heightened strength, endurance, or clarity. These meals are rarely encountered outside of significant events.

A party member can set out feast items for the benefit of the party, providing strong buffs before entering combat.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Feast Food and Drink Consumables
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}

##### <span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">- Exotic</span>
<span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">Exotic</span> consumables originate from distant lands, rare ecosystems, or unusual creatures. Their flavors, textures, and effects are often unfamiliar, sometimes unpredictable. These items may possess unique properties, such as enhancing senses, altering perception, or granting resistance to harsh environments. <span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">Exotic</span> foods are prized by explorers, collectors, and those seeking novel experiences.

<span style="color:rgb(163,53,238)">Exotic</span> food and drink provides unique effects reflecting the nature of the ingredients.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Exotic Food and Drink Consumables
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}

##### <span style="color:rgb(255,128,0)">- Mythic</span>
<span style="color:rgb(255,128,0)">Mythic</span> consumables are the subject of legend, created through rare circumstances or ancient recipes lost to time. These items possess extraordinary effects that surpass conventional nourishment, often tied to powerful magic or significant historical events. They may heal grievous wounds, restore vitality, or grant temporary supernatural abilities. <span style="color:rgb(255,128,0)">Mythic</span> fare is exceedingly rare and often sought after by heroes and rulers alike.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Mythic Food and Drink Consumables
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}

##### <span style="color:rgb(230,204,128)">- Divine</span>
<span style="color:rgb(230,204,128)">Divine</span> consumables transcend mortal creation, imbued with the essence of gods, celestial beings, or the fabric of creation itself. These items are unimaginably rare and carry effects that can reshape reality, alter destiny, or grant enlightenment. Their consumption is often accompanied by profound transformation, and in some cases, may fundamentally change the nature of the individual. <span style="color:rgb(230,204,128)">Divine</span> food and drink are typically encountered only in myth, sacred rites, or direct divine intervention.

> [!column|dataview 4]+ Divine Food and Drink Consumables
>  
{ .block-language-dataview}
