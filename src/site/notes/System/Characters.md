# [[System/Characters\|Characters]]

This archive documents the known individuals who inhabit and shape this world, from figures of great renown to those whose influence is felt only in quiet corners of history. Each character contributes, in ways large or small, to the unfolding narrative of kingdoms, conflicts, and cultures.

History is not written by events alone, but by the people who bring them to life. Every war begins with a decision, every kingdom rises through ambition, and every legend is carried on the actions of individuals.
